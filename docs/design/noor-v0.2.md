# Noor v0.2 · Play First, Learn Second

The owner approved this direction after the v0.1 child playtest. The child tended to skip reading, rush to buttons and seek interaction. Make the educational idea part of what the child does in the scene, with brief Arabic guidance available while playing.

- Take adventure journeys through the existing four worlds. Recommend a route and let children explore any eligible mission.
- Build interactive 2D scenes with drag and drop, tap alternatives and animations that explain what happened. Unlock locations and let children collect sourced knowledge treasures.
- Redesign gameplay with colorful cartoon art and use warm illustrated storybook art for maps and exploration. Keep Noor's lantern and branding.
- Keep existing lessons and progress available. Reuse approved curriculum and its stable IDs. New religious interpretations, visuals that add instructional details and treasures require review before child access.
