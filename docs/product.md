# Product definition — Noor / نور

Noor is a tablet-first, offline Arabic Islamic learning game for ages 6–8. Use short, clear MSA, large touch targets, responsive phone layouts and an expressive lantern companion. Keep rendering and motion light for low-end Android tablets. Physical QA starts on the owner's tablet, then moves to the child's low-end Android tablet for the release gate.

## Pilot and Season One

The weekend release is a playable pilot with a minimum of four approved micro-lessons, one per world. The owner provisionally accepted the initial 12-lesson outline for authoring. Every production record still requires its own source review, validation and final approval. [Curriculum roadmap](curriculum/roadmap.md)

Season One targets 60 micro-lessons, 180+ knowledge units and 600+ reviewed question variants. Aim for roughly 30 days of optional, engaging play at 15–30 minutes per day, including replay and review. Verify engagement through supervised playtests.

## Play loop

Learn a small idea, practice, receive an explanation and revisit it later. Let children choose among worlds and available activities. Support mixed quizzes, adaptive and spaced review, and practice after all lessons are finished. Track mastery by knowledge unit. Vary prompts to test recall and application. Give children time to answer and retry without a penalty. Flashcards use unscored self-assessment.

Use independently versioned offline packs and show What's New after a pack update. Offer optional knowledge treasures with verified bonus facts. Preserve earned history across updates. Show progress and reviewed sources in Parent Mode.

## Content scope

| World | Starter topics |
|---|---|
| الإيمان | معرفة الله، الشهادتان، أركان الإيمان |
| العبادة | أركان الإسلام، الوضوء، الصلوات الخمس |
| السيرة | النسب والمولد، نزول الوحي، الهجرة |
| القدوات | الخلفاء الراشدون، العشرة المبشرون بالجنة، الأئمة الأربعة |

Prioritize authentic and well-established topics. Teach clear obligatory and recommended practices, with advanced or disputed classifications deferred. Keep the app music-free. The authoring track supplies rights-cleared, reviewed narration and established memorization aids where appropriate. The durable lesson organization and targets live in the [Season One plan](curriculum/season-one.json).

## Success

A child can choose a world and complete a pilot lesson with little assistance. A parent can see learning progress and sources. Observe comfort and repeat engagement without collecting telemetry.
