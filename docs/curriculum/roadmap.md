# Season One curriculum roadmap

The playable pilot targets four approved lessons, one per world. Season One production targets are 60 micro-lessons, 180+ distinct knowledge units and 600+ reviewed question variants. Aim for roughly 30 days of optional play at 15–30 minutes per day, using replay and spaced review. Validate the estimate in playtests. [World allocations and unit targets](season-one.json)

The owner provisionally accepted the initial 12-lesson outline for authoring. The 79 ideas in the original outline are planning material. A final production record requires claim-level source verification, human review of its exact version and hash, schema validation and separate integration evidence.

1. Author and verify common, established fundamentals across the four worlds. Prioritize clear obligatory and recommended practices for children aged 6–8. Defer advanced and disputed classifications.
2. Review the playable pilot first. Organize the wider season as independently versioned packs. Keep source verification, approval, validation, integration and release QA visible as separate evidence.
3. Expand mixed quizzes and adaptive review with new variants keyed to stable knowledge units. Add optional verified knowledge treasures and rights-cleared established memorization aids when appropriate.
4. Record content releases and player-facing updates in [the release log](releases.json).
