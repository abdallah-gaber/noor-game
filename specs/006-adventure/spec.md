# SPEC-006 · W06 adventure vertical slice

Start with the [approved v0.2 direction](../../docs/design/noor-v0.2.md). The W06 slice will establish how children explore, discover a sourced idea through play and try it independently with their existing progress.

## 006-01 acceptance

- Given the approved W06 starter catalog and the developer preview route, the child sees a playable water courtyard immediately. Existing child routes retain the current lesson flow.
- When a child drags the water droplet onto the illustrated palms, or selects it and taps the palms, the water splashes, Noor reacts and replay becomes available. A drop elsewhere leaves the action available.
- The landscape scene also fits a portrait phone. Interactive objects have Arabic names and tap targets. Reduced motion retains visible feedback. Optional local effects respect the existing preference.
- The route fails closed when preview is disabled or the approved content is unavailable. The scene uses W06.K1, plays no speech and creates no progress or assessment records.

## 006-02 acceptance

- The debug preview opens on the palms and water droplet. Play continues through the selected W06 actions using the approved arrangement and concludes with stopping the flow at the tap.
- Each stop requires its scene interaction before revealing the next arrow. A missed drop or incomplete gesture keeps earlier actions in place and adds a visual and Arabic hint. Replaying starts the discovery again.
- Illustrated cues distinguish a splash from a wiping trail. Tapping works for every action, and reduced motion keeps the same feedback and completion gates.
- The scene uses the approved W06.K1–K3 records and W06.E01. The preview fails closed if its approved bindings are unavailable. Discovery creates no assessment or progress records.

## 006-03 acceptance

- The end of guided discovery offers the courtyard challenge. The player arranges five shuffled illustrated pieces using drag or tap, then submits the full order for grading against W06.E01. The water-conservation stop stays in discovery.
- A missed order preserves the attempt and offers an independent retry. After two misses, Noor highlights each next piece in turn. Assisted success completes the preview mission and stays distinct from independent correctness in saved evidence.
- Reopening the debug challenge restores the compatible shuffle, submissions and feedback or result. Replay creates a fresh round with the same preview completion key. XP is granted once for that key and the best star rating is retained.
- Discovery stays unscored. Preview progress, rewards and attempts use existing preview storage. The approved lesson experience and its saved history remain available as before.

## 006-04 acceptance

- In debug preview, the Worship map shows Noor, a recommended W06 fountain, a challenge marker and two future landmarks on winding paths. The future landmarks show an unavailable message and leave the player on the map.
- The fountain starts guided discovery and the challenge marker opens the ordering activity. Completing W06 offers a return to the map. A new run resumes an unfinished challenge or replays a finished one.
- The map reads the saved adventure completion and distinguishes a solve with Noor from an independent solve, including a later independent replay. The release and draft-review routes retain their existing approval rules.
- The illustrated map and focusable Arabic controls fit landscape and portrait, including enlarged text and reduced motion.

## 006-05 acceptance

- W06 completion makes one water-drop treasure available in preview. Opening the chest saves the reveal, celebrates with Noor and shows the approved water reminder. Reopening the collection or replaying W06 keeps one collectible.
- A chest marker on the Worship map opens the collection. The earned gem is visible there alongside the existing assisted or independent mission marker. Pending and already-opened chests restore from preview progress.
- W06 uses distinct offline foley for pickup, placement, washing, wiping, retry, finishing and opening the chest. The native player uses one stream and honors the audio preference.
- Animated feedback is short and local to the affected objects. Reduced motion keeps static feedback and the same controls. Production navigation, curriculum, grading and rewards retain their existing behavior.
