# SPEC-006 · W06 adventure vertical slice

Start with the [approved v0.2 direction](../../docs/design/noor-v0.2.md). The W06 slice will establish how children explore, discover a sourced idea through play and try it independently with their existing progress.

## 006-01 acceptance

- Given the approved W06 starter catalog and the developer preview route, the child sees a playable water courtyard immediately. Existing child routes retain the current lesson flow.
- When a child drags the water droplet onto the illustrated palms, or selects it and taps the palms, the water splashes, Noor reacts and replay becomes available. A drop elsewhere leaves the action available.
- The landscape scene also fits a portrait phone. Interactive objects have Arabic names and tap targets. Reduced motion retains visible feedback. Optional local effects respect the existing preference.
- The route fails closed when preview is disabled or the approved content is unavailable. The scene uses W06.K1, plays no speech and creates no progress or assessment records.

## 006-02 acceptance

- The debug preview opens on the palms and water droplet. Play continues through the selected W06 actions using the approved arrangement and concludes with stopping the flow at the tap.
- Each stop requires its scene interaction before revealing the next arrow. A missed drop or incomplete gesture keeps earlier actions in place and adds a visual and Arabic hint. Replaying starts the discovery again.
- Illustrated cues distinguish a splash from a wiping trail. Tapping works for every action, and reduced motion keeps the same feedback and completion gates.
- The scene uses the approved W06.K1–K3 records and W06.E01. The preview fails closed if its approved bindings are unavailable. Discovery creates no assessment or progress records.
