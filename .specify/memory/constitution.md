# Constitution — Noor / نور
- Child-first: Arabic simplified MSA, age 6–8; short, encouraging, readable lessons. No punitive streaks, ads, purchases, analytics, accounts or leaderboards.
- Tablet-first, responsive phone; RTL, accessible touch targets, no forced orientation; support both orientations where feasible. No music; optional non-musical effects and speech. No depictions of prophets or companions; Noor is an original stylized lantern.
- Religious integrity: every claim and question has traceable references; distinguish Qur’an, authenticated hadith and historical/fiqh scholarship. Common fundamentals first; disputed matters labeled with attributed views, never graded as universally wrong. No invented references. Draft material inaccessible in child mode until explicit human approval; qualified review for uncertain matters. Parent can inspect sources.
- Offline-first, local single profile, no child personal data in repository/evidence. Readable Arabic is the MVP baseline. Preserve accessibility semantics and optional speech infrastructure where practical. Optional narration or TTS must degrade gracefully and never download silently.
- SDD: specify, plan, tasks, implement, verify, review. Use focused verification per task and full regression, platform builds and comprehensive QA at SPEC boundaries. No completion without evidence. Generated dashboard derives from progress JSON, never edit HTML directly. Record decisions and blockers. Stop for approval on material scope or religious decisions.
- Delivery: use one feature branch and one pull request per specification. Verify and push each atomic task commit and confirm it is on the remote. Preserve task commits when merging into stable main with explicit owner approval.
- Launch a playable weekend pilot. Season One growth targets describe planned content production and optional engagement. Children may explore non-linearly, review, and keep playing after finishing the lesson catalog.
- Teach well-established Islamic knowledge and clear examples of obligatory and recommended practices for ages 6–8. Defer advanced or disputed classifications. Require final approval for each production record and its exact content hash. Provisional outline acceptance only authorizes curriculum development.
- Keep the app music-free. Curriculum authors supply any established, verified memorization aids, approved narration and explanations with verified reuse rights. Engineering does not create original poems, songs or religious mnemonics. Rights-cleared, reviewed content is the only source for production media.
